Legal / privacy

Privacy policy. Short, because there's not much to tell.

This site doesn't track you, run analytics or sell anything about you. Here's exactly what happens to the information you choose to share.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Who runs this site

teqqr.com is run by Rolando Scott, an independent engineer based in Costa Rica ("I", "me"). If you have any question about this policy or your information, email hello@teqqr.com.

What I collect

Very little. This site has no accounts, no contact forms, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it doesn't set cookies of its own. The information I end up with is only what you choose to send me:

  • Email. If you write to me, I receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.
  • Booking a consult. If you book a call, the name, email address and any notes you enter are collected by the scheduling service and shared with me so the meeting can happen.

Services this site relies on

  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site. Like any web host, it processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, mainly to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Cal.com provides the booking calendar. It only loads when you open it, and it may set its own cookies. See Cal.com's privacy policy.

How I use your information

Only to reply to you, schedule and hold the meetings you ask for, and carry out any work we agree on. I don't sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

How long I keep it

I keep correspondence for as long as it's useful for our conversation or working relationship, or as long as I'm required to for legal or accounting reasons. After that, I delete it.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete the information I have about you. Email hello@teqqr.com and I'll take care of it within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
