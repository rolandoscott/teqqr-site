Q01 Why not just hire an agency? agency vs direct +

If you need ten people in parallel, hire an agency — genuinely. For a migration, an AI build, or keeping a site healthy, most of what an agency adds is coordination overhead for work one experienced engineer does faster alone.

↳ see §04 / direct engagement

Q02 What if you get hit by a bus? continuity +

Everything runs in your accounts, in your repos, with documentation and runbooks written as I go. If I disappear tomorrow your team or a new contractor picks it up from the same notes I use.

↳ runbooks are part of every engagement

Q03 Do you work with our existing team? your team +

Usually, yes. I am most often the person who does the part your team does not want to own — the migration, the infrastructure, the AI piece — while they keep shipping features.

Q04 How do you charge? pricing +

Fixed price for defined projects, monthly for retainers. You see the number before the work starts, and it does not move unless the scope does.

↳ no discovery phase billed at senior rates

Q05 What size projects do you take on? scope +

From a single complex site to multi-site estates in the hundreds. The common thread is real stakes: traffic, compliance, or a downtime window measured in seconds.
